Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how Orca ("we", "us") handles personal data in connection with our extensions for Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce (Orca Celebrations and Orca Service Monitor), this website and our email support.

Our extensions

Our extensions run entirely on your own Magento installation. They do not send any data to Orca or to any third party, contain no analytics or tracking, and do not phone home for licensing or updates.

  • Orca Celebrations stores its effect settings and the images you upload in your Magento database and media folder. It sets no cookies and collects no data about your store visitors.
  • Orca Service Monitor stores the monitors you configure, their check results and incidents in your Magento database. Credentials you enter for monitored services are stored encrypted with your Magento encryption key. It only makes outbound requests to the services you choose to monitor, and sends alert emails through your store's own mail setup to the recipients you configure.

You, as the store owner, control all of this data. Uninstalling an extension with bin/magento module:uninstall --remove-data removes it.

Purchases

Our extensions are sold through the Adobe Commerce Marketplace. Adobe processes your purchase and payment under its own privacy policy. We receive only the order information Adobe shares with developers, and use it only to provide the extension and support.

Support emails

When you write to us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you send us (for example screenshots or log lines). We use it only to answer your request and keep it only as long as needed for that and for follow-up questions. We never sell or share it. Please do not send passwords, API keys or customer data.

This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We set no cookies and use no analytics. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. Write to orcashop.team@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
